Crowd sounds

The cheer-*.mp3 clips in this folder are cut, levelled and converted to
mono from "Free Crowd Cheering Sounds" by Gregor Quendel
(https://gregor-quendel.itch.io/free-crowd-cheering-sounds), licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

  cheer-soft-1.mp3  from "06 - Soft cheering - II", from 6.5s
  cheer-soft-2.mp3  from "05 - Soft cheering - I", from 8s
  cheer-mid-1.mp3   from "07 - Soft cheering and chatter", from 19.5s
  cheer-mid-2.mp3   from "09 - Ambience and cheering", from 13.5s
  cheer-big-1.mp3   from "04 - Strong cheering - II - Short", from 5.25s
  cheer-big-2.mp3   from "03 - Strong cheering - I", from 5.5s

murmur.mp3 is from "Cricket Stadium Cheering Sound (1 min)", supplied for
the game: 37s to 61s, mono, looped by crossfading its last 2.5 seconds into
its first. The pack's own ambience had voices in it that stood out.

Each cheer is eight seconds, faded out over its last two and a half; all are
levelled to -16 LUFS, and the game sets their loudness itself.
