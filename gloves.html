The new fists sit in the same groups and the same space on the handle and the wrist as the Classic's, so the grip, the wrists and the arms do not move; what changes is the shape of the fist and which way round each hand closes. Use Face, Back and Edge on the close-up to see it: from the flat face, the top glove shows its fingertips and thumb and the bottom glove its knuckles; from the back, the other way round; edge on, the fingers curling round the handle.
